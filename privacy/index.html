Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

Edwist Agent · Effective date · 生效日期：2026-10-16

English

Edwist Agent is an internal tool used only by authorized accounts to read and write their own Google Tasks. It requests the Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks.

  • We do not sell or share your Google user data with third parties.
  • Access tokens are stored only on controlled servers operated for Edwist.
  • You may revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
